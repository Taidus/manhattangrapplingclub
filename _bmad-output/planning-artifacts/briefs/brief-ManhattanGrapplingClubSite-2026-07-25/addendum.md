## Verified: Policy Content *(recovered from the three PDFs — replaces the PDF downloads)*

Source: GoDaddy CDN, `img1.wsimg.com/blobby/go/c82665ff-0c2d-45b5-91db-fba59f416f76/…`

**Community Standards & Policies** — an eight-point code of conduct: follow safety and hygiene rules and clean up after yourself; be punctual and minimize disruption; set and uphold your own boundaries and respect others' — consent is non-negotiable; hold yourself to the standard you hold others to; resolve conflict through honest communication; zero tolerance for sexual harassment, assault, racism, homophobia, transphobia, and all discrimination and abuse; the Club reserves the right to deny service to violators.

**Safety & Hygiene** — before training: shoes off and sandals off-mat, barefoot on mats, trimmed nails, clean body and gear, no jewelry, cuts sanitized and bandaged, no food or gum on the mats. Gear: wash after every practice, hang-dry the gi. Stay home for: skin infection or ringworm, fever or illness, an infected or badly bleeding wound. Safety: call 911 for emergencies, tell an instructor about blood on the mats, first-aid kits located throughout.

**Sparring Etiquette** — *Do:* ask anyone to spar, and accept that anyone may refuse; go slow if you are new; tap early and tap clearly (hand tap, foot stomp, or shout "TAP"); mind your mat space. *Don't:* feel obligated to spar or to continue; coach unless asked; strongarm smaller training partners; slam or twist out of tight positions — slamming is explicitly disallowed; lie down on an active mat area, rest on the sidelines instead.

### Full handout content *(supplied by the client as images, 2026-07-25 — supersedes the text-only extraction above)*

The client provided the three handouts as designed graphics. They are considerably richer than the text extraction: **each item is a headline, plus the reasoning, plus bolded key phrases.** That structure is what makes them readable, and the site now mirrors it.

Detail present in the graphics that the text extraction missed, all now on the site:

- Wrestling shoes **are** allowed on the mats; bare feet otherwise.
- Jewelry that can't be removed must be **covered with athletic tape**.
- Cuts are sanitized **again after training**, not only before.
- "If you or your gear are dirty or smelly, **you can't train**" — a stated refusal, not a suggestion.
- Hang-drying the gi is to **prevent shrinkage and reduce wear**, a reason the text version omitted.
- First-aid kits: members should **help themselves**, they are not gated.
- Sparring is organised as a **Do / Don't pair**, five each — not one flat list.
- "Slamming is **illegal in most rulesets**", not merely disallowed here.
- Standards item 5 (reflect on your own behavior as critically as others') and item 6 (willingness to compromise, adjust, forgive, or let be) were absent from the text extraction entirely.

### ⚠️ Error found in the client's own handout — needs correction at source

The **Safety & Hygiene** graphic gives the emergency address as:

> MANHATTAN GRAPPLE CLUB · 511 RICHARDS DRIVE · MANHATTAN, KS **65502**

**65502 is not a valid Manhattan, Kansas ZIP code.** Manhattan KS is **66502** (66503 and 66506 also serve the area). The Club's own website uses 66502, which is correct.

This matters more than a normal typo because it is printed on the sheet under **"Call 911 if there's an emergency. Our address is:"** — it is the address a panicking member would read aloud to a dispatcher. **The site uses 66502 throughout; the printed handout should be corrected.**

**Why this matters to the design:** this is the strongest trust content the Club owns. The consent language, the explicit right to refuse a roll, the tap-early instruction, and the zero-tolerance clause answer exactly the fears that stop a beginner — and especially a woman considering the Wednesday class — from walking in. It was invisible behind three PDF downloads. Surfacing it well is a conversion feature, not a compliance footnote.

**Provenance caution:** all three PDFs carry a personal name in their `Author` metadata, with `Producer: Canva`. *(Name redacted from this repository — see note at the end of this file.)* That is the name on the Canva account that made the documents. It is **not** evidence of who owns or teaches at the Club, and it must never be published as an instructor name. It was passed to the client privately as a lead to confirm or deny, nothing more.

## Verified: Brand Assets

`images/grapple club image.webp` — the club badge. A circular crest: black outer ring with "manhattan" in white script, a gold/amber sunburst center behind a black script "m" with a small red accent, "GRAPPLE CLUB" in a black banner across the bottom, and two red six-pointed stars flanking the center. White background, transparent-friendly.

Palette sampled directly from the badge pixels (not eyeballed) — 984×1015px, dominant-colour analysis:

| Role | Colour | Share of badge | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | `#000000` / near-black `#040706` | ~41% | Badge ring and lettering |
| Gold | `#FDB71B` | ~14% | Sunburst |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | ~19% | Badge field |
| Red | `#E81D2E` | <1% | The two six-pointed stars and the "m" flash |

Measured WCAG contrast, which dictates how these may be used:

| Pair | Ratio | Verdict |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink on Gold | 11.21 | Pass — **this is the primary button** |
| Gold on Ink | 11.21 | Pass — gold text is for dark surfaces |
| Gold on White | **1.76** | **Fails badly — gold text on light is banned** |
| White on Ink | 19.67 | Pass |
| Red on White | 4.52 | Passes body text, only just |
| Red on Ink | 4.35 | Fails body, passes large text only |

The decisive finding: gold is unusable as text on light backgrounds but excellent as a fill behind ink text. That single fact pushes the design dark-surface-first, which also matches the badge's own construction and suits a combat-sports gym.

This is a strong, sport-appropriate palette that the current site does not exploit. It should drive the whole design system rather than being confined to a logo in the header.

## Verified: Current Site Weaknesses (baseline for the redesign)

1. Stock hero imagery unrelated to jiu-jitsu.
2. No pricing on-site at all; cost only exists inside the Glofox portal.
3. No instructor or owner identified anywhere.
4. No phone, no email, and no on-site links to its Instagram or Facebook accounts (see *Verified: Social Profiles*).
5. No embedded map or directions affordance.
6. Community standards, safety/hygiene, and sparring etiquette gated behind three PDF downloads.
7. Schedule and "what to bring" live on separate pages.
8. Contact is a form only — no lower-friction channel.

Two implications for positioning:

1. **The instructor gap is a competitive liability, not just an omission.** The nearest competitor publishes named black belts and a verifiable lineage. An anonymous gym loses that comparison every time. This raises the priority of Open Question 2 from "nice to have" to "competitively urgent".
2. **The differentiators to lead with are the women's-only class, the free community open mat, and the beginner-first framing.** Note that Adventure Jiu-Jitsu runs "Women Empowered", so women's training is not unique in the wider area — but it is the only such class *in Manhattan proper* among the gyms found, and the Club's free no-drop-in-fee open mat has no equivalent anywhere in this set.
