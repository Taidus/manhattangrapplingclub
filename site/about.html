About Manhattan Grapple Club

Everyone is grappling with something. Here is who we are, how we coach, and who you will be training with.

01

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in Manhattan, Kansas

At Manhattan Grapple Club, we know that everyone is grappling with something. Work, stress, confidence, fitness, or simply the challenge of learning something new.

Our mission is to provide a place where members can leave those pressures at the door, step onto the mats, and become part of a supportive community.

We offer high-quality Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and grappling instruction for everyone, from complete beginners to experienced athletes. We are committed to excellent coaching, a welcoming and focused training environment, and continuing to learn and grow alongside our members.

Whether you want to develop new skills, sharpen old ones, build confidence, improve your fitness, or find a community, Manhattan Grapple Club has your back.

02

How We Coach

Four things that are true of every class on the schedule.

01

Beginners first

Every class is taught with beginners in mind. You do not need experience, and you do not need to get in shape before you start — we do jiu-jitsu to get in shape. Tuesday’s Introduction to Grappling is the easiest place to begin.

02

Consent is non-negotiable

You can ask anyone to spar, and anyone can refuse — including you, at any point, for any reason. Tap early, tap loudly. Our community standards put that in writing.

03

Gi and No-Gi, both

The club rotates between Gi and No-Gi training weekly, so you learn both games rather than specializing by accident. You do not need a Gi to start.

04

Open to the whole community

Sunday open mat is free and open to the entire local Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and grappling community — no drop-in fee, whatever gym you train at.

03

Who You’ll Train With

Coach Hunter

Purple belt

Hunter began training Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu in Chicago in 2016 as a way to stay active between tours as a drummer in rock bands.

Since then, he has earned his Purple Belt under Jeff Serafin and developed a passion for teaching. He continues to expand his knowledge by attending seminars and learning from some of the sport’s leading professional grapplers.

Hunter’s goal is to make jiu-jitsu approachable, practical, and rewarding for students at every experience level.
