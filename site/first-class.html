Your First Class

No experience, no fitness prerequisite, and nobody expecting you to know anything. Here is exactly how day one goes.

01

Day One

What your first class is actually like, start to finish.

1

Wear whatever you can move in

Comfortable clothing, athletic wear, preferably without plastic or metal. You do not need a gi to start.

2

You get a tour

A warm welcome and a quick tour — training mats, seating area, and restroom.

3

Nobody expects you to know anything

No experience required. Every class is taught with beginners in mind — and Tuesday’s Introduction to Grappling is the easiest place to start.

4

You do not need to be fit first

Not at all — we do jiu-jitsu to get in shape.

02

Questions

What do I wear to my first class?

Comfortable clothing, athletic wear, preferably without plastic or metal.

Do I need to have martial arts experience?

No experience required! Every class is taught with beginners in mind.

Do I need to be in shape before I start?

Not at all — we do jiu-jitsu to get in shape!

What should I expect when I walk in the door?

A warm welcome and a quick tour — training mats, seating area, and restroom.

Are there classes for beginners?

All classes are taught with beginners in mind, but Tuesday’s are best!

What is a Gi?

The Gi is the uniform traditionally worn for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu training.

Do you focus on Gi or No-Gi training?

Both! We rotate between Gi and No-Gi on a weekly basis.

Come train Tuesday.

Introduction to Grappling, 7:00pm. Wear something you can move in. That is the whole list.
