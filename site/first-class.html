New here?

Your First Class

No experience, no fitness prerequisite, and nobody expecting you to know anything. Here is exactly how day one goes.

01

Start Here

Four things to know before you walk in. That is the whole list.

  • Your free two-week trial begins with your first class.
  • Arrive a few minutes early, introduce yourself, and receive a quick tour of the facility.
  • Wear comfortable athletic clothing without metal, plastic, zippers, or hard fasteners.
  • You do not need a Gi or previous martial arts experience.

The easiest class to start with is Tuesday’s Introduction to Grappling at 7:00pm — though every class on the schedule welcomes beginners. Before you come in, it is worth reading how we train: consent, tapping early, and the safety and hygiene rules.

02

Questions

Do I need martial arts experience?

No. Every class is taught with beginners in mind.

Do I need to be in shape before I start?

Not at all. We do jiu-jitsu to get in shape.

What should I wear to my first class?

Wear comfortable athletic clothing without metal, plastic, zippers, or hard fasteners. You do not need a Gi for your first class.

What should I expect when I arrive?

You will receive a warm welcome and a quick tour of the training mats, seating area, and restroom.

Are there classes for beginners?

Yes. Every class welcomes beginners, but Tuesday’s Introduction to Grappling class is the easiest place to start.

What is a Gi?

A Gi is the uniform traditionally worn for Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu and Judo training.

Do you train Gi or No-Gi?

Both. The club rotates between Gi and No-Gi training weekly.

Still unsure? Call or email us before you come in, or read about the club and who you will be training with.
